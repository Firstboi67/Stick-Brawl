Kill enemies and walk over what they drop: coins 🪙, potions 🧪, better weapons and armor 🛡. A merchant visits after every 3rd wave (buy or try to steal), and bosses drop 2 chests. Rushers charge with a katana: jump over them (they stagger for 3s; they may stab and slam you mid-air), or parry with a well-timed swing and then tap 1 → 2 → 3 fast! Fire mages shoot fireballs: jump over them (they hit enemies behind you) or swing just as one arrives to reflect it. Swords have skills (G / F). Open the 🎒 bag to equip, and use the anvil in safe waves to upgrade or fuse duals. Keys: A/D move, W/Space jump, J attack, H potion, F storm, G katana skill.
